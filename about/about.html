RK GREEN ENERGY AND POWER SOLUTIONS LLP

Our Story

RK GREEN ENERGY AND POWER SOLUTIONS LLP. is a Customized Import House with Rich Import Experience. We are a leading name in the Electric Vehicle (EV) industry, serving as a one-stop solution for all your electric vehicle needs. Established in 2021, our company has grown to become a trusted importer of high-quality EV components and solutions.

We are basically customized EV parts Supplier at India. We can help OEM from Scratch to End to develop a right product as OEM need. We also arrange Homologation Support to OEM with best Quality product at Reasonable price.

Driven by innovation and sustainability, we are committed to accelerating the global transition towards clean and efficient mobility. With a strong network of partners and clients worldwide, we provide reliable EV parts, ensuring superior performance and durability.

Our expertise covers a wide range of electric vehicle segments, making us a preferred choice for businesses, manufacturers, and individuals looking for dependable EV solutions under one roof. We deals in Electric two wheelers and three wheelers and electric golf cards and their spare parts.

Our Services

Customized EV Component Supply

End-to-End OEM Support

We partner with Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs) to provide complete support from product ideation to launch. Our services include product development, prototyping, & sourcing.
